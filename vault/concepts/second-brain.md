# Summary

A second brain is a personal knowledge system outside your head. Tiago Forte's CODE workflow describes the life of a note: Capture what resonates, Organize it for action, Distill it to its essence, Express it as output.

# Key points

- The value is in retrieval and reuse, not in collecting.
- Plain files you own outlive any one app or model.
- The expensive steps (organising, distilling, linking) are the ones people skip, and the ones an agent can do.
