# Summary

Sixty to 100 episodes is the sweet spot; series under 40 tend to underperform on freemium apps, and series over 100 risk a mid-season drop-off around episodes 60 to 80.[^filmustage-script]

| Episodes | Phase |
| --- | --- |
| 1 to 10 | Establish characters and the core conflict, with two or three emotional peaks |
| 11 to 40 | Deepen the conflict, add subplots, raise the stakes of near-misses |
| 41 to 70 | The dark middle: things get worse, resolution is furthest away |
| 71 to end | Resolve in order of introduction, central arc last |

# Key points

- Map the emotional peaks, the paywall, the darkest point and the final payoff before writing scenes.[^filmustage-script]
- Even the finale should leave something open for a second season.[^filmustage-script]
