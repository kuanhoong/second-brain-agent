# Summary

The cliffhanger is the last shot of the episode and its job is to make the next one unmissable. Four types cover nearly every ending: a reveal, a threat, a revelation and a decision.[^invideo-hooks]

# Key points

- End one beat before the resolution.[^invideo-hooks] Cut earlier than feels safe and do not explain the cliffhanger.[^filmustage-script]
- Storyboard the final frame as a vertical still and approve it before generating video.[^invideo-hooks]
- A held close-up on a face is a reliable final frame: the expression is the hook.[^morphic-closeup]
- The paywall episode needs the strongest cliffhanger in the season.[^storyflow-script]
