# Summary

Most platforms put the paywall around episode 10, so the first ten episodes decide whether a viewer converts.[^filmustage-script] A common shape puts the first paywall near episode ten, and the cliffhanger there must be the strongest so far.[^storyflow-script]

# Key points

- Treat the free episodes as a complete emotional argument for paying.[^filmustage-script]
- Pose the big question at the threshold and land the major revelation one or two episodes after it.[^filmustage-script]
- Check the target app's current free-episode count before you plan. It differs by platform and changes.[^bamboo-paywall]
