# Summary

Every line must push the conflict or show a character under pressure. Cut small talk, setup lines and exposition.[^filmustage-script]

# Key points

- Characters act on feelings rather than explain them: they deny, deflect or act, and the viewer infers.[^filmustage-script]
- Keep lines short. Save longer speeches for a confession or reveal at the end of an episode.[^filmustage-script]
- Script reaction beats explicitly: where they look, what their hands do, what they almost say.[^filmustage-script] These become the [expression prompts](/prompting/emotion-and-expression-prompting.md).
- Keep location description minimal; the vertical frame is mostly faces.[^filmustage-script]
- Write so the episode still reads with captions and no sound ([captions](/production/captions-and-sound-off.md)).
