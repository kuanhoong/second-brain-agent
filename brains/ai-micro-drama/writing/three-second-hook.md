# Summary

Every episode opens on a hook of about three seconds: a face, a shock line or a reveal already in motion.[^invideo-hooks] From episode 2 onwards the opening reconnects with the last cliffhanger and reframes it as new tension, rather than recapping it.[^genra-short-drama]

# Key points

- Freeze-frame test: pause at second three. If the image needs backstory to make sense, the hook is not working.[^filmustage-script]
- Generate the cold-open shot at the highest quality you can afford; it is the shot that earns the rest of the episode.[^invideo-hooks]
- Episode 1 hooks usually open on an injustice: the humiliation, the betrayal, the stolen inheritance.[^oakgen-tropes]
