# Summary

China's first dedicated regulation for the format, the NRTA's measures on micro-drama development, took effect on 1 September 2026. It covers series with episodes under 20 minutes, and an AI-generated micro drama must carry a visible notice in every episode.[^remio-nrta]

# Timeline

- 1 April 2026: filing rules for AI comic-style dramas became fully mandatory.[^zaikei-china-rules]
- June 2026: the NRTA issued classification standards for AI micro-dramas; projects costing RMB 800,000 or more, or touching sensitive topics, count as "key" micro-dramas.[^kucoin-nrta-tiers]
- 1 September 2026: the measures took effect.[^remio-nrta]

# Key points

- The audience is huge: 664 million micro-drama viewers in mainland China.[^zaikei-china-rules]
- A widely reported claim says 98.7% of a surveyed group of AI titles failed to break even; it describes that sample, not the whole market.[^remio-nrta]
