# Summary

Fully AI-generated micro drama with realistic people falls squarely inside platform labelling rules. TikTok requires a label on AI-generated content with realistic images, audio or video; YouTube requires creators to disclose realistic altered or synthetic content.[^creatorsagency-disclosure]

# Key points

- From May 2026 YouTube also uses its own signals to find and label AI content that creators did not disclose.[^techwyse-youtube]
- Since 21 July 2026 TikTok requires a disclosure label on ads with realistic AI-generated content before they can run.[^stellar-tiktok-ads]
- Veo outputs carry an invisible SynthID watermark.[^google-veo31]
- Turn the label on at upload rather than waiting for a platform to apply it.
