# Summary

AI makes it trivial to put a real person's face or voice into a drama, and regulators and platforms are acting on it. China's draft rules on AI copyright treat stealing a performer's likeness through voice cloning or face swapping as a violation; ByteDance's Hongguo app removed hundreds of videos in Q1 2026 that relied on stolen AI likenesses.[^sixthtone-likeness]

# Key points

- Actors are being offered small sums to license their faces: one actress was offered 500 yuan for a one-year licence.[^lds-one-person]
- Invent your characters. Do not prompt for a look-alike of a real actor or public figure.
- Only clone a voice with written permission, and keep the record.
- Keep the casting portraits and prompts that produced each character, so you can show the face is original.
