# Summary

A micro drama is a scripted serial made for a phone held upright: 9:16 frame, episodes of roughly one to two minutes, and dozens of episodes per season, each ending on a cliffhanger.[^filmthreat-format] An AI micro drama is the same format where the shots come from generative video models instead of a physical shoot, with AI also used to draft and break down the script.[^invideo-definition]

The format is a conversion funnel as much as a story: free opening episodes hook the viewer, and later episodes are unlocked one at a time.[^bamboo-paywall] In China, AI has become the main production engine for it.[^36kr-tiered]
