# Summary

A series bible (also called a show bible) is the single reference the writers and, in AI production, the prompt writers work from. Typical sections are title and author, logline, introduction, themes, structure, tone, setting, characters, story, episodes and future plans.[^wedio-show-bible]

# For AI micro drama, add

- A **visual style block**: one paragraph of look, palette, lighting and film texture that is pasted into every prompt ([prompt template](/templates/video-prompt-template.md)).
- A **location list** with one approved reference image per location.
- The **character sheets** and **voice choices** for every recurring character ([character sheet](/characters/character-sheet.md), [voice casting](/characters/voice-casting.md)).
- The **episode grid** with the paywall row marked ([episode grid](/writing/episode-grid.md)).

# Key points

- Keep it short. A bible is read under time pressure; one producer's advice is very short character bios.[^footnotes-bible]
