# Summary

A character bible holds everything you need to know about a character: motivation, personality, physical traits, backstory and family.[^storm-character-bible] Its job is consistency: nobody's eye colour changes mid-season unless the story says so.[^storm-character-bible]

# Fields that matter most in micro drama

- **Want and wound.** What they chase in episode 1 and why.
- **Secret.** Give each character a secret nobody else knows; it drives reveals.[^footnotes-bible] In micro drama the secret is often the trope itself (the hidden heir, the secret billionaire).
- **Power position** at the start and how it flips across the [season arc](/writing/season-arc.md).
- **Voice:** two or three verbal habits, and the [cast voice](/characters/voice-casting.md).
- **Why they are in the story.** If you cannot say, cut them.[^storm-character-bible]

The visual side lives in a separate [character sheet](/characters/character-sheet.md).
