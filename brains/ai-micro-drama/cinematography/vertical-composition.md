# Summary

Compose for the phone the episode plays on. Put the face in the top third, keep the bottom third visually quiet for captions, and keep action away from the caption band, the engagement buttons on the right edge and the status bar.[^invideo-vertical]

# Key points

- Layer depth vertically: foreground low in frame, subject above it, environment behind.[^invideo-vertical]
- Single subjects, tight two-shots stacked in depth, and close framing on faces and hands suit the format.[^pexo-make]
- Export at 1080x1920. The vertical frame is the format, so no landscape variants are needed.[^pexo-make]
- Approve vertical framing on stills, where corrections are cheap, before you animate.[^invideo-vertical]
