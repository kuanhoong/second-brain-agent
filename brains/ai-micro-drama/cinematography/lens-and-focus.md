# Summary

Lens words change how a shot feels. Google's guide lists shallow depth of field, wide-angle lens, soft focus, macro lens and deep focus.[^google-veo31]

# Key points

- **Shallow depth of field** separates a face from a busy background; it is the default for close-ups.
- **Long lens (85mm and up)** flattens and isolates; good for portraits and for spying on a character from a distance.
- **Wide-angle** exaggerates space and distorts close faces; use it for unease, not for the romantic lead.
- **Rack focus** shifts attention from one plane to another, for example from the heroine to the rival behind her.
- **Macro** for the extreme close-up of the ring, the pill, the signature.
