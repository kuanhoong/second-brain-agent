# Summary

Lighting sits in the style-and-ambiance part of the prompt formula.[^google-veo31] Name the light source and its quality, not just a mood word: "lit by the harsh fluorescent overhead lights and the green glow of the monitor" does more than "moody".[^google-veo31]

# Common setups

- **High key:** bright, even, few shadows. Romance, comedy, the office.
- **Low key:** deep shadows, one hard source. Thriller, revenge, the mafia boss.
- **Motivated practicals:** light that comes from something in frame (a lamp, a phone screen, a fireplace).
- **Golden hour / blue hour:** warm or cold natural light for emotional peaks.
- **Neon or colour contrast:** nightlife, danger, fantasy.

# Key points

- Keep a series look: write it once in the [series bible](/characters/series-bible.md) style block and reuse it.
- Lighting continuity matters across a cut; a light that jumps sides between two shots breaks the scene.[^storyblocks-continuity]
