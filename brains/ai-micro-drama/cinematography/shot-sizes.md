# Summary

Shot size is how much of the subject the frame holds. Video models understand the standard terms: wide shot, medium shot, close-up, extreme close-up.[^vertex-veo-guide]

| Size | Frames | Use in micro drama |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Extreme close-up (ECU) | Eyes, a hand, a ring, a phone screen | The detail that turns the scene |
| Close-up (CU) | Face and shoulders | The workhorse: dialogue, reactions, cliffhangers |
| Medium close-up (MCU) | Chest up | Dialogue with a little gesture |
| Medium shot (MS) | Waist up | Two characters, a confrontation |
| Full shot (FS) | Head to toe | Entrances, the makeover reveal |
| Wide shot (WS) | Person in a setting | Rare: establishing a location |

# Key points

- Close-ups dominate vertical drama because a face fills the tall frame and a wide shot wastes it.[^morphic-closeup]
- Establish a place through details rather than scope.[^pexo-make]
