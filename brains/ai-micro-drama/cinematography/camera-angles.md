# Summary

Camera angle is where the camera sits relative to the subject. Veo's prompt guide gives eye level, low angle, top-down and aerial views as examples of camera positions it understands.[^vertex-veo-guide]

| Angle | Reads as | Micro-drama use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eye level | Neutral, intimate | Default for dialogue |
| Low angle (looking up) | Power, threat | The CEO, the villain, the heroine after her comeback |
| High angle (looking down) | Weakness, isolation | The humiliation scene |
| Overhead / top-down | Detached, trapped | A body on the floor, a desk of evidence |
| Dutch (tilted) | Unease | Betrayal discovered |
| POV | Seeing through a character | Reading the text message, finding the photo |

# Key points

- When prompting a high angle, describe what the camera sees beneath the subject; for a low angle, describe what is behind or above them.[^ltx-veo]
- Flip the angle when power flips. The same character shot from below in episode 40 and from above in episode 2 tells the arc without words.
