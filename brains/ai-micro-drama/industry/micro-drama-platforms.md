# Summary

DramaBox and ReelShort were the top-grossing short drama apps in Q1 2026, each with close to $140 million of in-app revenue, while challengers such as NetShort grew fast.[^sensor-tower-2026] ReelShort belongs to Crazy Maple Studio and leads the market outside China; DramaBox reported $323 million of revenue and a $10 million net profit for 2024.[^vitrina-platforms]

# Key points

- ReelShort leads on share outside China, followed by DramaBox, DramaWave and NetShort.[^mpa-reelshort]
- Platforms commission and license content by genre and territory, so a pitch should name the target app and its audience.[^vitrina-platforms]
- TikTok, Instagram Reels, Snapchat Spotlight and YouTube Shorts are major distribution channels for vertical drama as well.[^filmthreat-format]
