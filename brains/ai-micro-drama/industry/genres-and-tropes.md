# Summary

Micro-drama runs on high-concept tropes that can be understood in seconds: revenge, rebirth, hidden heirs, contract marriage, the cold CEO, mafia romance and fated-mate fantasy. Each works because episode 1 can open on an injustice and end on a reveal.[^oakgen-tropes]

# Key points

- ReelShort's first viral hit was a werewolf romance, *Fated to My Forbidden Alpha*; the second was *The Double Life of My Billionaire Husband*, a secret-billionaire story.[^asiatechlens-hits]
- Western versions localise Chinese templates, for example moving a campus romance onto a hockey team.[^conversation-tropes]
- In China the genres are diversifying into family, personal growth, education, martial arts and time travel.[^chinadaily-levels-up]
- One Chinese producer puts 65 to 70% of a project's success down to the script.[^nbc-minidramas]
