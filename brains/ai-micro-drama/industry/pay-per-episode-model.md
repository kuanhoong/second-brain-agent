# Summary

Most apps give away the opening episodes, often the first 8 to 12, and put the paywall mid-story at a cliffhanger. Viewers then pay per episode with coin bundles, and some apps let them watch ads to unlock the next one.[^bamboo-paywall] The cliffhanger is therefore part of the payment flow, not only a storytelling device.[^bamboo-paywall]

# Key points

- The paywall episode carries the strongest cliffhanger so far, because it has to sell the rest of the season.[^storyflow-script]
- Writing implication: the free episodes must make a complete emotional case for paying. See [Paywall Episode](/writing/paywall-episode.md).
- Advertising is still a small revenue stream but growing; Media Partners Asia expects it to reach about 15% of ReelShort's revenue by 2028.[^mpa-reelshort]
