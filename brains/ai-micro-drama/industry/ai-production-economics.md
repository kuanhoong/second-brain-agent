# Summary

AI collapses the cost and time of a micro-drama, and volume has exploded as a result. About 128,000 micro-dramas launched in China in Q1 2026, and over 95% of them were AI-generated.[^36kr-tiered] Over the whole first half of 2026, 367,000 were released and AI productions made up more than 74%.[^technode-h1]

# Reported costs and timelines

- Judian Short Dramas reports AI-assisted production of 100,000 to 150,000 yuan per drama, roughly a third of the traditional cost.[^vitrina-ai-companies]
- Korean platform Vigloo made an AI drama in six weeks at 90% lower cost.[^vml-ai]
- A three-person team reportedly shipped a 10-episode series in three days at about $1,000 per episode. This is the vendor's own figure.[^invideo-vertical]

# The catch

Cheap production means a flood of titles. By early 2026 more than 10,000 AI micro-drama titles were released in China each month.[^vml-ai] Most do not earn their money back, which is one reason China tightened the rules in 2026 ([China's rules](/distribution/china-micro-drama-rules.md)).[^remio-nrta]
