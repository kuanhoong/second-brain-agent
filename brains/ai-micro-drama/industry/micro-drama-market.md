# Summary

Micro drama is a multi-billion-dollar format. Omdia estimates global revenue of $11 billion in 2025, growing to $14 billion by the end of 2026, of which about $3 billion comes from outside China; the US is now the largest international market.[^omdia-market] Media Partners Asia sizes the market outside China at $2.7 billion in 2025 and $3.6 billion in 2026.[^mpa-reelshort]

# Key points

- Short drama apps passed 850 million downloads in Q1 2026, up 140% year on year, with about $750 million of in-app revenue.[^subscription-insider]
- Outside China the market is a five-player race led by ReelShort (about 29% share), then DramaBox (21%), DramaWave (13%) and NetShort (10%).[^mpa-reelshort]
