```
[CAMERA, own sentence]   Vertical 9:16 frame. [Shot size], [angle], [one camera move or "locked-off camera"].
[LAYOUT]                 [Subject] in the top third; [what fills the middle]; lower third kept plain for captions.
[SUBJECT]                [Identity block from the character sheet.]
```

# Worked example

> Vertical 9:16 frame. Close-up, eye level, locked-off camera. Mia's face in the top third, the lit phone in her hand low in frame, lower third kept plain. Mia, a 26-year-old woman with a shoulder-length black bob and a small scar above her left eyebrow, in a grey hotel uniform. She reads the message; her jaw tightens, her eyes stay dry, she slowly turns the phone face down. A hotel staff kitchen at night, steel counters. Cool fluorescent light from above, warm spill from the phone screen, shallow depth of field, cinematic, subtle film grain. SFX: a single phone buzz. Ambient noise: the low hum of a fridge. No one else is in the kitchen.

Attach Mia's character sheet and the kitchen reference when the model supports reference images ([reference image prompting](/prompting/reference-image-prompting.md)).
