One page per episode. Write the timestamp skeleton before any dialogue. Background: [episode structure](/writing/episode-structure.md).

```
EPISODE [N] - "[title]"            Target length: [75] s     Paywall episode: [yes/no]
Previous cliffhanger: [one line]

[0:00-0:03]  HOOK
  Picks up / reframes: [how the opening continues the last cliffhanger]
  Opening image: [a face / a shock line / a reveal in motion]

[0:03-0:45]  FRICTION
  INT./EXT. [LOCATION] - [DAY/NIGHT]
  [Action line: what we SEE. Keep it to one or two lines.]

          [CHARACTER]
     (reaction beat: where they look, what their hands do)
     [Short line.]

[0:45-1:05]  SPIKE
  [The reversal / new evidence / POV flip. Must work with the sound off.]

[1:05-1:15]  BUTTON
  Final frame: [the image we cut on, one beat before the answer]
  Cliffhanger type: [reveal / threat / revelation / decision]
  Question the viewer is left with: [one line]
```

# Checklist

- [ ] The freeze-frame at second three makes sense without backstory
- [ ] At most two or three characters on screen
- [ ] Every line pushes the conflict
- [ ] The episode reads with captions and no sound
- [ ] It ends on a question, not an answer
