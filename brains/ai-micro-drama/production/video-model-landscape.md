# Summary

As of late 2026 the main video models are Google Veo 3.1, Kuaishou Kling 3.0 and ByteDance Seedance 2.0, with Runway and open models alongside. OpenAI discontinued Sora 2: the app on 26 April 2026 and the API on 24 September 2026.[^rangy-compare]

| Model | Known for | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Veo 3.1 | Native audio, prompt adherence, reference images | 4, 6 or 8 second clips; 9:16 output; SynthID watermark[^google-veo31] |
| Kling 3.0 | Multi-shot storyboards, element references | Up to 15 seconds; multilingual audio[^fal-kling3] |
| Seedance 2.0 | Reference-heavy generation, unified audio and video | Up to 9 images, 3 videos, 3 audio clips per call[^fal-kling-seedance] |

# Key points

- Prices and versions change monthly. Re-check before you budget a season; this note has a short shelf life.
- Most teams use more than one model and route by shot type ([shot routing](/production/shot-routing.md)).
