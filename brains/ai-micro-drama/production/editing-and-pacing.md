# Summary

The edit is where the episode's rhythm is made. Cut tight, caption for sound-off viewing, and design a thumbnail that sells the hook.[^morphic-make]

# Key points

- Change the picture every two to three seconds (a new angle, a reaction shot, a push-in) to reset attention. This is a vendor's rule of thumb.[^genra-short-drama]
- Cut the cliffhanger before the resolution; if the viewer feels satisfied, the cut came too late.[^genra-short-drama]
- Keep two deliverables apart: the clean platform episode with plain subtitles, and social cutdowns with their own hook, captions and cover frame.[^kolbo-deliver]
- If you upscale, burn captions after the upscale, never before.[^kolbo-deliver]
