# Summary

A storyboard-first workflow plans every shot as an approved visual panel before any video model runs.[^invideo-storyboard-first] Fixing a still is cheap; fixing a video generation is not.

# Key points

- Generate panels from the shot list with the locked character sheets attached, then animate the approved panels ([image-to-video](/prompting/image-to-video-prompting.md)).
- Approve vertical framing on stills before animation.[^invideo-vertical]
- Storyboard the cliffhanger frame of every episode as a vertical still and approve it first.[^invideo-hooks]
