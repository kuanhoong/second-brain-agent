# Summary

Treat sound as four layers: dialogue, sound effects, ambience and an emotional score. Native-audio models can generate the first three inside the clip.[^google-veo31]

# Key points

- Prompt sound effects and ambience explicitly with `SFX:` and `Ambient noise:`.[^google-veo31]
- Generated clips each come with their own ambience; level it in the edit so the room does not change sound at every cut.
- Use a consistent score theme per character or relationship; it carries emotion for viewers who have the sound on.
- Only use music you have the rights to.
